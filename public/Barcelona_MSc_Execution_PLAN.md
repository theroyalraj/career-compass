## Phase 2 — Loan Application (June–August 2027)

**Goal:** Get SBI Global Ed-Vantage sanctioned and disbursed in time for Barcelona arrival.

> **⚠️ CRITICAL:** SBI sanctioning takes 15–25 days after complete documents. Disbursal can add another 7–10 days. Work backwards from your September Barcelona arrival — you need sanction by mid-July at latest.

### 2.1 Choose your SBI branch correctly

- [ ] Do NOT go to your nearest SBI branch — only **designated B-category branches** handle Global Ed-Vantage (abroad loans above ₹7.5L with collateral)
- [ ] Find nearest designated branch: call SBI toll-free 1800 11 2211 or use WeMakeScholars (sbieducationloan.wemakescholars.com) — they handle SBI applications free of charge
- [ ] Schedule an appointment; walk-in queues at loan counters are extremely slow

> **Tip:** WeMakeScholars is a free service that acts as an intermediary between you and SBI. They know the right branches, review your documents for errors before submission, and follow up on your behalf. Highly recommended — dozens of Indian abroad students use them every year.

### 2.2 Prepare complete SBI document package

**Student documents**
- [ ] Passport (valid min. 18 months from start of course) — original + 2 self-attested copies
- [ ] Aadhaar card — original + 2 self-attested copies
- [ ] PAN card — original + 2 self-attested copies
- [ ] UPF Admission letter (official PDF from UPF) — 2 copies
- [ ] UPF Fee structure / Cost of Attendance letter — 2 copies
- [ ] All academic mark sheets: 10th, 12th, all semester mark sheets of B.Tech/BE — originals + 2 copies each
- [ ] Graduation certificate / provisional degree certificate — original + 2 copies
- [ ] Current employer letter on company letterhead (confirming employment, role, salary)
- [ ] Last 3 months salary slips — original + 2 copies
- [ ] Last 6 months bank statements (salary account) — original + 2 copies
- [ ] 2 recent passport-size photographs
- [ ] Language test scorecard (if UPF required IELTS/TOEFL) — 2 copies

**Co-applicant documents (parent / guardian)**
- [ ] PAN card — original + 2 self-attested copies
- [ ] Aadhaar card — original + 2 self-attested copies
- [ ] ITR (Income Tax Return) — last 2 financial years + acknowledgement — 2 copies
- [ ] Form 16 — last 2 years — 2 copies
- [ ] Last 3 months salary slips (if salaried) — 2 copies; OR business registration proof + IT returns (if self-employed)
- [ ] Last 6 months bank statements — 2 copies
- [ ] 2 recent passport-size photographs

**Collateral documents (if pledging property)**
- [ ] Original property documents / title deed — keep originals, bring to branch
- [ ] Latest property tax receipt — original + 2 copies
- [ ] Encumbrance certificate (EC) for last 15 years — get from sub-registrar office
- [ ] Approved building plan (if urban/municipal property)
- [ ] NOC from all co-owners of property — get this signed early
- [ ] ⬜ Property valuation — SBI will arrange; takes 5–7 days after document submission
- [ ] ⬜ Legal opinion — SBI will arrange; takes 5–7 days after valuation

> **⚠️ CRITICAL:** The EC (Encumbrance Certificate) can take 3–5 working days at the sub-registrar office. Start this immediately — do not wait.

### 2.3 Loan application steps

- [ ] **Day 1:** Submit complete document package to SBI branch / WeMakeScholars
- [ ] **Day 1–3:** SBI reviews documents; expect 1 round of additional requests ("deficiency list")
- [ ] **Day 3–5:** Address all deficiencies; resubmit
- [ ] **Day 5–12:** Property valuation (SBI-arranged) + legal opinion (SBI-arranged)
- [ ] **Day 12–20:** Credit appraisal and credit committee review
- [ ] **Day 20–25:** Sanction letter issued ← target this by **15 July 2027**
- [ ] **Post-sanction:** Accept sanction terms; execute loan agreement; submit original property documents
- [ ] **Disbursement:** First tranche released; usually within 7–10 days of agreement signing

### 2.4 If SBI takes too long — parallel Credila application

- [ ] If SBI has not given a sanction letter by 10 July 2027, immediately apply to HDFC Credila (credila.com)
- [ ] Credila approval: 3–5 days (no collateral needed, faster process)
- [ ] Accept Credila as backup; withdraw if SBI comes through in time
- [ ] Note: Credila rate is 12–13% vs SBI 9.5%; use only if SBI timeline blows up

> **Real case — Rahul T., Delhi University → Universitat Pompeu Fabra 2021:**
> Rahul applied to SBI in late June for a September start. SBI ran into delays with EC and property valuation. He applied to Credila on July 10 as backup; Credila approved in 4 days. SBI came through on July 22. He cancelled Credila (no penalty at pre-disbursal stage) and went with SBI at 9.5%. Lesson: apply to both, cancel the one you don't need.

### 2.5 Loan amount to apply for

Use the loan calculator widget (utkarsh_loan_calculator.html) for your specific numbers. As a reference:

| Scenario | Apply for |
|---|---|
| No scholarship | ₹22–24L (total need + ₹2L buffer) |
| JN Tata ₹5L | ₹17–19L |
| JN Tata + UPF waiver ₹10L total | ₹12–14L |
| Inlaks / Erasmus (fully funded) | Pre-sanction only as backup; don't disburse |

- [ ] Finalise loan amount based on scholarship decisions received by June 30
- [ ] Apply for recommended amount + ₹2L buffer (you can disburse less than sanctioned amount)

**Goal:** Spain student visa (D-type) approved and in hand before departure.

### 3.1 Spain student visa requirements

- [ ] **Proof of enrolment** — UPF admission letter (conditional or unconditional)
- [ ] **Proof of sufficient funds** — bank statements showing coverage for the study period; this is where your SBI sanction letter or first disbursal receipt is critical
- [ ] **Accommodation proof** — booking confirmation for Barcelona accommodation (hostel / shared flat / university housing) for at least the first 1–2 months
- [ ] **Travel insurance** — must cover Spain + Schengen zone; min coverage €30,000; valid for full study period
- [ ] **Medical certificate** — not older than 3 months; apostilled in India
- [ ] **Criminal background check** — from local police; apostilled
- [ ] **Passport photos** — 2 recent, white background
- [ ] **Visa fee** — approximately €80 (check current rate at Spanish consulate website)
- [ ] **Filled visa application form** — from Consulate of Spain in India

> **⚠️ CRITICAL:** Book your visa appointment at the Spanish Consulate (Mumbai/Chennai/Delhi/Kolkata) as early as possible. Peak season (July–August) for student visas to Spain can mean 4–6 week wait for an appointment.

> **Reference:** Spanish Consulate India: exteriores.gob.es (check the consulate covering your state)

### 3.2 Apostille documents

Spain requires several documents to be apostilled (Indian government attestation):
- [ ] Educational certificates (degree, mark sheets)
- [ ] Medical certificate
- [ ] Criminal background check
- [ ] Get apostille from Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) office — takes 3–7 working days per document

### 3.3 Accommodation in Barcelona

- [ ] **University housing** — check UPF housing portal; usually fills fast
- [ ] **Shared flats (pisos compartidos)** — Idealista.com, Fotocasa.es, Uniplaces.com
- [ ] Budget: €500–900/month for a room in a shared flat (Gràcia, Poblenou, El Born neighbourhoods near UPF Poblenou campus)
- [ ] Book at least 2 months' accommodation before visa appointment — you need the booking confirmation for the visa
- [ ] Avoid signing a long lease before arriving — book for 1–2 months, then find permanent place in person

> **Real case — Sneha P., IIM Bangalore → UPF (exchange 2023):**
> Sneha used Uniplaces for a 2-month booking at €650/month near Ciutadella Park. Once in Barcelona, she found a permanent room in a 4-person flat in Poblenou for €580/month through a Facebook group ("Erasmus Barcelona housing"). In-person searching is much more effective for Barcelona housing than booking from India.

### 3.4 Pre-departure financial setup

- [ ] Understand SBI disbursement: SBI can transfer directly to your Spanish bank account, or to your Indian account (then you transfer via Wise to Spain)
- [ ] Open a Spanish bank account after arriving — Openbank (Santander's digital bank), N26, or Revolut work well; some require your TIE (residency card) to activate fully

### 4.2 Legal registration (TIE — Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero)

This is your Spanish residency card — **required for almost everything** (bank account, renting a flat, some contracts).

- [ ] **NIE first** — Número de Identificación de Extranjero: go to Extranjería (immigration office) or a Comisaría (police station); need: passport, visa, 2 photos, form EX-15 (filled), fee ~€10 (tasa)
- [ ] **TIE application** — book appointment (cita previa) at Oficina de Extranjería; can take 2–4 weeks to get an appointment, so book immediately on arrival
- [ ] TIE takes 4–6 weeks to process after appointment
- [ ] Enrol at local Ayuntamiento (town hall) — Empadronamiento (municipal registration) — needed for many services; just passport + accommodation contract

> **⚠️ CRITICAL:** Citas previas (government appointments) in Barcelona are in extremely high demand. Book your TIE appointment on Day 1 in Spain — the system opens at midnight and slots disappear within minutes. Use cita.mpt.gob.es

### 4.3 Banking

- [ ] Open Openbank or N26 account (digital, minimal paperwork, no minimum balance)
- [ ] Transfer first month's living expenses from India via Wise (~0.5% fee vs 3–5% via SBI SWIFT)

## Phase 5 — During Study Year (October 2027 – September 2028)

**Goal:** Excellent academic performance + strong audio/ML portfolio + early career positioning.

### 5.1 Part-time Work — When, What, and Financial Impact

**When can you legally start working?**

Spain's long-stay student D-visa allows up to 30 hours/week of work. You do NOT need a separate work permit, BUT you must have your **TIE (Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero)** in hand first. TIE takes 4–6 weeks to process after arrival. Realistic start date: **Month 2–3 (November/December 2027)**.

**Work options ranked by hourly rate:**

| Type | Rate | Hours/wk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Remote backend consulting (Toptal/Upwork) | €25–40/hr | 10–15 | Best-paid; uses your Java/Spring Boot expertise |
| Audio/ML freelancing | €15–30/hr | 10–15 | Builds portfolio; harder to find initially |
| Online tutoring (programming, music) | €15–25/hr | 5–10 | Preply, Superprof; flexible schedule |
| UPF Research Assistant (audio/ML lab) | €9–12/hr | 10–15 | Lower pay but huge career value; ask professors Month 1 |
| Curricular internship (registered with UPF) | €800–1,200/month | Up to full-time | Best for CV; apply December 2027 for Feb 2028 start |
| On-campus work (library, lab monitor) | €9–11/hr | 5–10 | Easiest to get; start here in Month 2 |

**Financial impact scenarios:**

| Scenario | Hours | Rate | Monthly income | 9-month total | Loan reduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 10/wk | €12/hr | €480/mo | €4,320 → ₹4.1L | ₹3–4L less loan |
| Moderate | 15/wk | €15/hr | €900/mo | €8,100 → ₹7.7L | ₹7–8L less loan |
| Optimistic (tech) | 15/wk | €25/hr | €1,300/mo | €11,700 → ₹11.1L | ₹10–11L less loan |

**Why this is the most powerful financial move available:**

At the optimistic scenario, you earn enough to cover all Barcelona living costs during the study year. This means your SBI loan goes only toward tuition + setup (₹7.2L), not living. Your effective loan drops from ₹22L to ~₹7–8L, and your 10-year EMI drops from ₹28,700/month to ~₹10,000/month.

> **Real case — Arjun M., NIT Surathkal → TU/e Netherlands 2022:**
> Arjun did remote Java consulting for a former Indian employer at €25/hr, 10 hours/week from Month 3. He earned ~€9,000 over 9 months. His loan need dropped from ₹18L to ₹10L. Monthly EMI: ₹14k vs ₹25k — a difference of ₹11k/month for 10 years = ₹13.2L in lifetime EMI savings.

**Checklist for part-time:**
- [ ] Month 1: Email UPF audio/ML faculty about Research Assistant openings
- [ ] Month 1: Update Upwork/Toptal profile with your Java/Spring Boot + ML transition story
- [ ] Month 2: Apply for on-campus work (library, lab) once TIE appointment booked

- [ ] Month 5: Apply for semester 2 curricular internship (deadline typically December/January)

## Phase 7 — Post-Graduation Financial Management (October 2028+)

**Goal:** Start EMI on schedule without financial stress; build net worth in EUR.

### 7.1 EMI strategy

- [ ] EMI begins 6 months after course end — approximately **April 2029** (if course ends Oct 2028)
- [ ] At SBI 9.5%, ₹22L loan, 10-year tenure: approximately ₹28,700/month (~€300/month at €1=₹95)
- [ ] At €40k/year net salary in Spain (~€2,100/month net): EMI = ~14% of net take-home — **manageable**
- [ ] Consider pre-paying 1–2 extra EMIs per year using year-end bonus; SBI allows partial prepayment without penalty (confirm with your branch — terms vary)

### 7.2 Tax implications

- [ ] Indian income on Indian accounts while abroad: understand FEMA rules; do not let NRO/NRE status lapse
- [ ] Spain: declare Indian bank accounts if balance exceeds €50,000 (Modelo 720 declaration)
- [ ] Consult a tax advisor (CA in India + gestor in Spain) in Year 1 — ~€200–300 total cost, worth it to avoid penalties

### 7.3 Currency risk

- [ ] EMI is in INR (₹); your salary is in EUR; EUR/INR rate matters
- [ ] If EUR strengthens: your EMI burden decreases in EUR terms
- [ ] If INR strengthens significantly: consider converting some EUR to INR and pre-paying loan
- [ ] Do not hold large INR amounts idle — put in FD or liquid fund in India earning 6–7%
