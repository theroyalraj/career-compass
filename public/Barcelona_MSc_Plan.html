Phase 0 Foundation — Get Organized May 2026 · Do this week
🟡 In Progress ▾

  • Request a formal "Cost of Attendance" letter from UPF International Office — SBI requires this
  • Build a master document folder (7 sub-folders) backed up on Google Drive
  • Have a family conversation about collateral: residential property = 9.15–9.5% SBI rate vs. no collateral = 12% Credila
  • Start EC (Encumbrance Certificate) process at sub-registrar office — takes 3–5 working days
📌 Real case — Karan S., IIT Kharagpur → UPF SMC 2024

Karan submitted the UPF email confirmation to SBI instead of the formal PDF admission letter. The branch rejected his application. He had to request the PDF directly from UPF (10 days) and restart the process — losing nearly 3 weeks. Get the PDF on Day 1.

⚠️ Critical: SBI does not accept agricultural land as collateral. Residential or commercial property only. Verify this now before planning around it.
Phase 1 Scholarships — Reduce the Loan May–August 2026
🟡 Start Now ▾
  • Inlaks Scholarship — Up to $120,000 (fully funded). Deadline: check inlaksfoundation.org. Prepare 2-page SOP + 2 academic references.
  • JN Tata Endowment — ₹5–10L at 0% interest (effectively free money vs SBI's 9.5%). Apply at jntataendowment.org
  • UPF Fee Waiver — Email UPF admissions; ask about partial tuition waiver or RA positions during study year
  • PM Vidya Lakshmi — Government portal for subsidized loans (vidyalakshmi.co.in); worth applying as supplement
  • Every ₹1L you don't borrow saves ~₹20–25k in total interest over 10 years
📌 Real case — Aditya R., BITS Pilani → UPF SMC 2022

Aditya got JN Tata ₹5L (0% interest) + UPF waived €500 tuition for academic merit. His SBI loan dropped from ₹18L to ₹12.5L. At 9.5% over 10 years, this saved him ~₹10k/month in EMI.

📌 Real case — Neha K., NIT Trichy → KTH Stockholm 2023 (Erasmus Mundus)

Neha received a full Erasmus Mundus scholarship: tuition paid + €1,400/month stipend. She invested her Indian savings in PPF + FD during study year. She graduated debt-free with ₹6L more in savings than when she left.

⚠️ Critical: SBI sanction must be in hand by mid-July for a September start. Work backwards. Missing this means paying Credila's 12% rate for the entire loan tenure — that's ₹3–5L extra over 10 years.

Financial Multiplier

Part-time Work: The Hidden Lever

Part-time income during your MSc year is the highest-leverage financial move available to you. It can reduce your loan by ₹8–12L and cut monthly EMI significantly.

📅
When Can You Start?
Legally, once your TIE (residency card) is in hand — typically 4–6 weeks after arriving in Barcelona. Practical start: Month 2–3 (November/December 2026). Spain's long-stay student D-visa allows up to 30 hours/week of work — no additional permit needed with TIE.
💡
Why It's a Game-Changer
At €20/hr × 15hr/week, you earn €1,200/month — enough to cover all living costs. Over 10 months, that's ~₹11.4L you don't need to borrow. This alone can cut your loan from ₹22L to ₹11L, halving your monthly EMI from ₹28k to ₹14k.
